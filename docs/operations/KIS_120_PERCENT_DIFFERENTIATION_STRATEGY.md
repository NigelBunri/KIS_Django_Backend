# KIS 120 Percent Differentiation Strategy

Status: Phase 30 foundation.

Purpose: define what makes KIS more than a bundle of WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube, Coursera, Amazon, Apple Health, Discord, and Bible app patterns. This document prepares the next stage without enabling risky features before the 80% launch cut and 95% parity evidence are complete.

The default production state is:

```text
KIS_LAUNCH_CUT_MODE=80
KIS_PARITY_95_FEATURES_ENABLED=False
KIS_DIFFERENTIATION_120_FEATURES_ENABLED=False
KIS_EXPERIMENTAL_120_FEATURES_ENABLED=False
```

## Differentiation Principles

- KIS is Christian-first, family-safe, and dignity-preserving.
- The app should feel like a royal, calm, premium place for worship, learning, commerce, care, work, and community.
- Differentiation must build on reliable 80% launch behavior and 95% category parity, not bypass it.
- Child/youth safety, pastoral review, anti-pornography media safety, privacy, trust, and USD-only monetization safety are non-negotiable.
- Advanced AI, recommendations, public web, live provider calls, embeds, and growth loops stay disabled until evidence exists.

## Staged Implementation Slices

### 120-A: Spiritual Growth OS

- Build from Bible plans, meditations, reminders, notes, highlights, KCAN/publisher tools, and notification badges.
- Add spiritual journey templates, family devotional mode, pastoral review metadata, and offline scripture proof.
- Do not enable public sharing of child/youth journey data.

### 120-B: Kingdom Impact Dashboard

- Build privacy-safe summaries for personal, partner, institution, education, health, commerce, and ministry impact.
- Use aggregates only; do not expose private health, payment, verification, or raw audit data.
- Include anti-vanity and anti-manipulation copy rules.

### 120-C: Creator Institution Ecosystem

- Connect channels, courses, shop/service offerings, partner workspaces, verified institutions, and Bible/KCAN publishing into safe journeys.
- Keep all payments USD/direct-provider-first.
- Keep promotional credits non-cash, non-transferable, non-withdrawable, and not exchange-rated.

### 120-D: Family-Safe Recommendations

- Expand the privacy-safe recommendation foundation with age modes, Christian safety ranking, and blocked/muted/hidden exclusions.
- Require child/youth safe defaults before any personalized public surface.

### 120-E: Live Experience Layer

- Prepare live ministry, learning, commerce, and health Q&A provider adapters.
- Keep provider calls disabled until staging evidence proves moderation, replay safety, child-safety controls, and rollback.

### 120-F: Christian AI Companion

- Extend AI assistance placeholders with redaction, role-specific prompts, pastoral/safety limits, and staff audit visibility.
- Keep live AI calls disabled by default.

### 120-G: Global Low-Bandwidth Excellence

- Prioritize offline scripture, messaging reliability, education caching, channel thumbnail fallbacks, and low-data media selection.
- Validate on device-lab low-bandwidth profiles before enablement.

### 120-H: Royal UX Memory System

- Extend Royal UX 2.0 with age-aware interaction density, simplified modes, calmer notifications, and highly polished gold/deep-purple surfaces.
- Validate text contrast, tap target size, and older-user readability.

## Launch Evidence Criteria

Every 120% slice needs:

- owner and release ticket;
- explicit feature flag;
- 80% launch-cut compatibility proof;
- 95% parity dependency proof where applicable;
- child/youth, Christian principles, media safety, privacy, and security review;
- iOS/Android device-lab proof for user-facing changes;
- rollback path;
- no open P0/P1 blocker;
- clear production go/no-go status.

## Default No-Go Conditions

- Any feature requires live AI, live media-safety provider, live verification provider, live streaming provider, public indexing, public embeds, or referrals without staging evidence.
- Any feature exposes raw documents, raw storage paths, private health/payment/verification data, decrypted message bodies, or secret values.
- Any feature weakens anti-pornography rules, Christian principles, child/youth safety, or USD-only monetization safety.
- Any feature breaks existing 80% launch APIs/UI or 95% parity commitments.
