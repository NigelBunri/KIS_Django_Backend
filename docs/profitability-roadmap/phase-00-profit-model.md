# KIS Profitability 80%+ Roadmap - Phase 00 Profit Model Audit

Date: 2026-05-16

Status: Completed as a planning/audit phase. No runtime behavior changed.

## Executive Position

KIS should not monetize like a single-purpose social app. The highest-profit path is to operate KIS as a Christian ecosystem platform where free consumer participation creates reach, and creators, institutions, shops, health providers, education providers, partners, ministries, and verified organizations pay for growth, trust, commerce, management, analytics, and distribution.

The revenue model must stay legally conservative:

- KIS promotional credits are non-cash, non-transferable, non-withdrawable, and not exchange-rated.
- New paid workflows should be USD/direct-provider first, with Flutterwave as the first payment provider where already planned.
- Business, institution, creator, promotion, verification, and transaction-fee revenue should carry the profitability load.
- Basic Christian community value should remain free enough to grow network effects.

## Profitability Thesis

KIS can reach 80%+ profitability readiness if it prioritizes:

1. Institution subscriptions.
2. Creator/channel paid tools.
3. Commerce transaction fees and seller tools.
4. Education course revenue share.
5. Health provider subscriptions and booking fees.
6. Partner workspace subscriptions.
7. Verification/trust revenue.
8. Featured placement and Christian-safe promotions.
9. Retention loops that make KIS useful daily.
10. A profitability command center that tracks revenue, conversion, churn, and provider costs.

## Core Assumptions

- KIS is a Christian app and must preserve Christian principles, family safety, anti-pornography rules, and child/youth protections.
- Free social usage alone will not create strong early profit.
- Institutional users have a higher willingness to pay than ordinary users because KIS can help them acquire members, customers, students, patients, followers, and trust.
- KIS should monetize “growth and management value,” not basic community participation.
- The fastest revenue path is manual acquisition of paying institutions/creators before large-scale automated growth.
- KIS should avoid legal risk by keeping credits promotional and avoiding any cash-like wallet behavior.

## Free Versus Paid Boundaries

### Should Stay Free

- Basic account/profile.
- Basic messaging.
- Basic Bible reading.
- Viewing public content/channels.
- Following/subscribing to free channels.
- Basic community participation.
- Basic marketplace browsing.
- Basic institution discovery.
- Basic verification badge visibility.

### Should Become Paid Or Limited

- Multiple managed channels.
- Channel analytics beyond basic counts.
- Scheduled publishing.
- Paid/private channel content.
- Live streaming beyond trial limits.
- Institution dashboards.
- Team roles and permissions.
- Partner workspace management.
- Seller/product promotion.
- Course selling and certificate issuance.
- Provider booking/payment management.
- Featured placement.
- Advanced public embeds.
- Advanced AI assistance.
- Enterprise audit/export/admin controls.

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Creator Revenue

Possible offers:

- Creator Pro monthly/annual plan.
- Channel Growth plan.
- Paid channel memberships.
- Paid live events.
- Paid content/courses.
- Embed analytics.
- Sponsored content tools with required labels.

Recommended early pricing:

- Creator Pro: USD 5-15/month.
- Creator Growth: USD 20-49/month.
- Paid content/live/course commission: 5-15%.

### 2. Institution Revenue

Possible offers:

- Shop Pro.
- Education Institution Pro.
- Health Provider Pro.
- Partner/Ministry Workspace Pro.
- Verified Organization plan.
- Branch/team/member management.
- Landing page builder.
- Analytics and trust summary.

Recommended early pricing:

- Starter Institution: USD 10-25/month.
- Growth Institution: USD 49-99/month.
- Enterprise/Network: custom.

### 3. Transaction Fees

Applies to:

- Marketplace orders.
- Service bookings.
- Education courses/cohorts/certificates.
- Health bookings where legal.
- Paid live events.
- Partner event tickets.

Recommended early fee:

- 3-8% for commerce/health/service bookings.
- 5-15% for courses, digital content, and creator products.

### 4. Verification Fees

Applies to:

- Shops.
- Partners/companies.
- Health institutions.
- Education institutions.
- Ministry/KCAN publishers.

Recommended model:

- User verification: free or low-cost.
- Institution verification: paid processing fee.
- Annual renewal or reverification fee for high-trust categories.

### 5. Promotion Fees

Applies to:

- Featured channels.
- Featured courses.
- Featured products/services.
- Featured health providers.
- Featured partner organizations.
- Featured Bible/ministry content.

Guardrails:

- Sponsored/promoted labels must be visible.
- Christian-safe ad policy must reject pornography, gambling, predatory finance, exploitative health claims, and manipulative content.
- Promotion should be curated and trust-preserving.

### 6. Consumer Premium

Possible offers:

- Family Plus.
- Spiritual Growth Plus.
- Bible journey premium.
- Advanced family safety and guided journeys.

Recommended early pricing:

- USD 3-10/month.

Consumer premium should not be the first revenue dependency because conversion is usually lower than institution/business conversion.

### 7. Enterprise Revenue

Targets:

- Church networks.
- Ministries.
- Schools.
- Clinics and care networks.
- Christian business networks.
- NGOs.
- Diaspora communities.
- Kingdom-focused organizations.

Features:

- Multi-branch management.
- Roles and permissions.
- Audit logs.
- Data export.
- Custom branding.
- Private partner network.
- Dedicated onboarding/support.

Pricing:

- Custom annual contracts.

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Too many features before one revenue funnel works | High | Start with creator/institution/payment/promotion funnels. |
| KIS credits interpreted as money | High legal risk | Keep credits promotional only and remove exchange/withdraw/transfer language. |
| Charging ordinary users too early | Medium-high | Keep core community/Bible/messaging free. |
| Health monetization creates regulatory exposure | High | Monetize booking/admin/trust, not diagnosis. Add disclaimers and legal review. |
| Ads damage Christian trust | High | Use curated promoted placement and strict Christian-safe ad policy. |
| Provider/payment failures | High | Add audit logs, reconciliation, rollback, and incident runbooks. |
| Institutions do not understand value | High | Add dashboards showing reach, leads, bookings, orders, members, students, and revenue. |
| Low retention | High | Build daily Bible, notification, channel, course, order, appointment, and partner loops. |

## 80%+ Profitability Readiness Criteria

KIS should not be considered 80%+ profitability-ready until:

- At least three monetization engines are live and testable:
  - institution subscriptions;
  - creator/channel plans;
  - commerce/education/health transaction fees or direct payment flows.
- Flutterwave USD payment flows are proven in staging and production.
- KIS credits are confirmed non-cash and non-transferable in code, UI, and docs.
- Pricing and upgrade screens exist for creator/institution/partner plans.
- Verification fee workflow is documented and gated.
- Promotion/featured placement policy exists.
- Revenue dashboard tracks MRR, transaction volume, paid accounts, churn, failed payments, refunds, and provider fees.
- Legal/compliance review is completed for health, credits, refunds, subscriptions, promotions, and child safety.
